Keep mobile holograms static with no new idle animation frames; pause desktop highlights during interaction and honor reduced motion. Effects are derived from academic records and never award progress.

Specialization courses stay in their dedicated specialization constellation. Actual terms, failures, retakes and corrections remain in grade history and the journal summary; never duplicate stars or move descendants to new constellations.

CIVINCO is a separate review dimension reached through the black-hole portal, not a gate that unlocks CELE. Retain its access rule: a confirmed specialization, every shared and selected-path technical course previously activated (ACTIVE, COMPLETED or FAILED), and no more than 24 currently ACTIVE technical units. Mastery is not required. Preserve old assessment history without using it to unlock CELE. CIVINCO areas are MSTE, PSAD (including construction), HGE; CELE uses MSTC, PSAD, HGE. Wait for supplied CIVINCO files before populating review sets or topic mappings. Ordinary student activations are capped at 21 ACTIVE technical units, including simultaneous lecture partners and retakes. Allow up to 24 only when the confirmed path has at most 24 unfinished required units (including failed courses). Count all open technical enrollments, including old-term and transferred-branch records; changing the journey term does not free capacity. Do not erase or reject historical completed grades because of these caps. New active booklet imports use the same cap. Non-major Taken records do not track active enrollment. Verified Admin view remains unrestricted.
