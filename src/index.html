A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Skill tree help

The shared sky includes all four years. Year buttons travel smoothly to each year; they don’t change your academic year. Set your actual year in My journal.

Inspect any course, follow its prerequisite links, and explore every region. Shared courses form everyone’s foundation. From Year 3, confirm your self-reported specialization before activating its courses.

Each constellation has its own color. Use the specialization checks to reveal one branch and its paths on the shared sky; selecting it again hides it. Click a warm dashed prerequisite line to trace its connected courses. Course details distinguish hard prerequisites, soft prerequisites, and co-requisites.

The academic journey meter fills as you complete required courses. Previewing another branch does not change it. Academic completion is self-reported. Mastery quizzes and course descriptions are reserved for a later phase. The CIVINCO assessment and Hardmode review screens have their architecture in place, with assessments still to come.
